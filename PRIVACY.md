# Vesper privacy policy

Vesper is a personal notes app. It has no accounts of its own, no server, no ads and no analytics, and it collects nothing about you.

**Your data stays with you.** Your notes, tasks, ideas, plans, photos and videos are stored on your own devices.

**Google Drive sync is optional.** If you turn it on, Vesper signs in with your Google account and uses only the `drive.appdata` permission: a hidden folder in your own Drive that only Vesper can use. Vesper cannot see any of your other Drive files. Everything is encrypted on your device with a passphrase you choose before it is uploaded, so neither Google nor anyone else can read it. The passphrase is never stored or sent anywhere.

**Removing your data.** RESET SYNC in the app deletes Vesper's files from your Drive. Uninstalling the app removes its local data from that device.

**Sharing.** Vesper never sends your data to its developer or to anyone else. Its use of Google data is limited to syncing your own notes between your own devices.

**Questions** can be asked through the issues page of this repository.
