# Vesper privacy policy

Vesper is a personal notebook app. It has no accounts of its own, no server, no ads and no analytics, and it collects nothing about you.

**Your data stays with you.** Your notes, tasks, goals, daily candles, Bible cards, Grimoire posts (text, photos and videos) and alarms are stored on your own devices. If your phone's own Android backup is turned on in its settings, Android may include Vesper's data in that backup, as it does for other apps.

**Google Drive sync is optional.** If you turn it on, Vesper signs in with your Google account and uses only the `drive.appdata` permission: a hidden folder in your own Drive that only Vesper can use. Vesper cannot see any of your other Drive files. Everything is encrypted on your device with a passphrase you choose before it is uploaded, so neither Google nor anyone else can read it. The passphrase is never stored or sent anywhere. Grimoire posts and alarms are not synced.

**Notifications and permissions.** Vesper shows a notification only when an alarm you set is ringing. It has no reminders and plays no sounds other than the alarm itself. The alarm permissions (exact alarms, notifications, full-screen alarm, starting after a reboot, vibration) are used for that and nothing else. Internet access is used only for Google Drive sync.

**Removing your data.** RESET SYNC in the app deletes Vesper's files from your Drive. Uninstalling the app removes its local data from that device.

**Sharing.** Vesper never sends your data to its developer or to anyone else. Its use of Google data is limited to syncing your own data between your own devices.

**Questions** can be asked through the issues page of this repository.
